# Upper Colorado River Basin

Interactive map of the Upper Colorado River Basin above Lees Ferry:
https://daveedge1.github.io/Upper-Colorado-River-Basin/

- **Rivers** are drawn in proportion to mean annual natural flow (water years 1906–2020).
  Hover a river for its name.
- **Natural-flow gauges**: the 20 Reclamation natural-flow gauges at or above Lees Ferry,
  with mean flow, share of Lees Ferry flow and the annual record.
- **Reservoirs**: storage-capacity circles at the dams; lake outlines appear when zoomed in.
  Capacities are rounded full-pool totals entered by hand; verify before citing.
- **Tree-ring sites**: species, elevation, chronology span, and correlation of each
  chronology with water-year natural flow at Lees Ferry and in seven source subbasins.

Data sources: Reclamation natural flows (November 2022 release); NHDPlus V2 flowlines and
NHD waterbodies; USGS NLDI drainage basins; USGS 3DEP elevation.

This repository holds only the built page. It is generated and updated automatically
from the project's analysis repository; edits made here will be overwritten.
